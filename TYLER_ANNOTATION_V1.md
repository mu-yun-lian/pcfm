# Tyler Cowen Dual-Annotation Contract v1

## Operational claim

Given a verified `tyler-source-v1` artifact, the module:

1. selects every and only `needs_human_annotation` post;
2. creates two blind packets with the same candidate set and frozen codebook;
3. accepts only complete, separately signed A/B submissions;
4. validates every label against cited authored-prose unit IDs;
5. recomputes exact full-label agreement and Cohen disposition kappa;
6. exposes only disagreements to a separate adjudicator;
7. refuses a final dataset when fixed reliability floors or adjudication
   completeness fail.

Passing this contract establishes procedural reproducibility only. It does not
establish that a label is true, that a public statement is a private belief, or
that the five axes are a complete model of Tyler Cowen.

## Frozen label structure

Every candidate receives exactly one disposition:

- `clear_in_scope_stance`
- `clear_out_of_scope_stance`
- `not_a_stance`
- `uncertain_attribution`
- `insufficient_context`

Every label must cite at least one authored-prose unit. A
`clear_in_scope_stance` must additionally provide one or more registered
axis/direction pairs. Other dispositions must not carry axis directions.

Directions are:

- `toward_pole_1`
- `toward_pole_2`
- `mixed_or_conditional`

Annotator confidence is `low`, `medium`, or `high`. It describes confidence in
the annotation, not Tyler Cowen's confidence in the statement.

## Registered axes

| Axis ID | Pole 1 | Pole 2 |
|---|---|---|
| `ai_acceleration_vs_risk_regulation` | AI acceleration | risk regulation |
| `market_mechanisms_vs_government_intervention` | market mechanisms | government intervention |
| `technological_progress_vs_employment_displacement` | technological progress | employment-displacement protection |
| `state_capacity_vs_individual_liberty` | state capacity | individual liberty |
| `short_term_social_cost_vs_long_term_growth` | accepting short-term social cost | protecting against short-term cost |

The axis ID fixes the semantic order. A direction cannot be interpreted without
the frozen codebook digest carried by the packet.

## Evidence and role rules

- Packet A never contains B labels; packet B never contains A labels.
- A and B must be signed by different verifier IDs and must target their own
  packet slots.
- Every packet item must be labeled exactly once. Partial submission,
  cherry-picking, extra candidates, and cross-packet replay are refused.
- Evidence citations may reference only authored-prose unit IDs in that item.
- Source titles and URLs are context, not evidence units.
- The adjudicator may resolve only A/B disagreements and cannot alter agreed
  labels.
- Labels and free-text rationales are never generated by an LLM in this stage.

Software isolation is not physical isolation. An external study operator must
ensure annotators do not communicate and do not see model outputs.

## Fixed reliability floors

- minimum packet size: 5 candidates;
- exact full-label agreement: at least 2/3;
- Cohen kappa on disposition: at least 0.40;
- two distinct valid annotator signatures;
- 100% candidate coverage;
- 100% disagreement resolution before finalization.

These floors are constants and have no CLI or configuration override.
Adjudication cannot rescue a packet that failed the pre-adjudication
reliability floors; the codebook must instead be revised on discovery evidence
and evaluated on a new untouched packet.

## Temporal roles

- publication through 2024-12-31: `training_discovery`;
- 2025-01-01 through 2025-12-31: `protocol_validation`;
- 2026-01-01 through 2026-07-31: `retrospective_diagnostic`;
- later records: refused until bound to a separately registered prospective
  study.

The current real RSS packet contains 2026 retrospective diagnostic records.
Even unanimous annotations therefore produce zero training-eligible records.

## Null and alternatives

The null is that the codebook does not yield reproducible labels. Alternatives
to genuine clarity include label-prevalence agreement, shared annotator bias,
shared misattribution of quoted material, strategic public rhetoric, omitted
context, and an axis language that excludes the actual stance.

## Refusal states

The module refuses missing or ineligible candidates, source or codebook
mismatch, duplicate or incomplete coverage, invalid evidence, invalid label
shape, same annotator, wrong slot, signature failure, low agreement, unresolved
disagreement, old schema, artifact tampering, or unregistered future evidence.
